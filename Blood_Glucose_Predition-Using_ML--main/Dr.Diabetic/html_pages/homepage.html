About our project

Technology Meets Health Awareness

The Diabetic Management System brings together a machine-learning prediction interface and educational resources to demonstrate how technology can support diabetes awareness.

Our goal is to make health-related information easier to explore while clearly distinguishing educational predictions from professional medical assessment.

Learn About Us

Important health information: This website is an educational project. Its machine-learning outputs are not validated medical diagnoses and should not be used to make treatment decisions. Always consult a qualified healthcare professional for personal medical advice.
