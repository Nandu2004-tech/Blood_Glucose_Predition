About Our Project

Empowering people with diabetes through health education, useful resources, and machine learning technology.

Our Story

The Diabetic Management System is an educational project designed to explore how technology and machine learning can support diabetes-related information and awareness. Our goal is to create a simple, accessible platform where users can explore resources and interact with a research-based prediction model.

Our Vision

We aim to make diabetes-related educational information easier to explore through a user-friendly digital platform. We believe technology can help people learn, ask better questions, and have more informed conversations with qualified healthcare professionals.

Our Values

  • Empowerment: Encourage informed health discussions.
  • Education: Make diabetes-related information easier to understand.
  • Accessibility: Build a simple, responsive website.
  • Innovation: Explore machine learning for educational purposes.
  • Responsibility: Communicate model limitations honestly.

About the Team

This project brings together web development, Python, and machine learning to demonstrate how software can present health-related information in a clear and accessible format.
